📌 Project: QClosed
📄 Description:
QClosed is an automation bot designed to handle bookings where quality closures are required. It involves UI-based automation to interact with internal platforms and complete closure actions.
✅ Enhancements / Work Done:


Updated UI selectors and automation logic to accommodate changes in the front-end elements, ensuring continued reliability of the bot.


Expanded booking coverage by refining logic to handle more complex or previously unhandled cases.


Integrated encrypted logging to improve data protection and audit readiness.


Upgraded supporting packages for improved performance, stability, and compatibility.


🎯 Impact:


Maintained high reliability despite evolving UI changes.


Enhanced automation coverage and reduced manual overhead.


Improved security through encrypted logs.

----- Dupe

Originally designed to handle duplicate bookings caused by technical errors or repeated user actions, the Dupe Cancellation bot focused on automatically cancelling one of the duplicate bookings and closing the associated Compass case.
✅ Enhancements / Work Done:


Redesigned the bot POC to shift the focus from cancelling bookings to directly closing Compass cases, increasing the bot’s applicability and reducing unnecessary cancellations.


Achieved a 90% success rate by improving logic and expanding coverage across more booking scenarios.


Delivered an FTE benefit of 2, reducing manual intervention and improving team efficiency.


Integrated log encryption to enhance bot data security and compliance.


Updated existing packages to streamline the process and improve overall maintainability.


🎯 Impact:


Broader booking coverage and improved resolution handling.


Significant performance uplift and reduced operational load.


Strengthened security posture with encrypted logging.
