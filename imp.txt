In our current flow, “expert” does not always mean a human agent. We have multiple AI experts like Trip Planner, Flight, Baggage, Policy, etc., so “chat with expert” can still be interpreted as routing to the right AI expert.

Human handoff should trigger only when the user clearly asks to connect with a real person/human agent/live support.

So if the expected behavior is to treat “chat with expert” as human handoff, we need to explicitly add this phrase as a human-handoff trigger in the guardrail/routing logic.
