FPCLOUD is our internal cloud portal that acts like a Google Maps or Yellow Pages for all company software and infrastructure. It brings together everything—applications, APIs, databases, services, and even virtual machines—into one easy-to-use place. For each item, it clearly shows who owns it, what it does, where its documentation lives, and how it connects to other systems. This means developers and operations teams no longer have to jump across multiple tools, ask colleagues, or dig through scattered docs; instead, they can quickly discover what they need, understand dependencies, troubleshoot issues faster, and even create or manage new services and VMs with guided workflows. In short, FPCLOUD saves time, reduces confusion, and makes the whole software ecosystem inside the company easier to navigate and manage.
