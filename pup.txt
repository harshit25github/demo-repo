Easy (1–6)

“I’m visiting Paris for the first time. What are the top 10 must-see places?”

“Suggest attractions in Dubai that are good for first-time visitors.”

“What are the best places to visit in Goa for a 2-day trip?”

“I’ll be in Delhi for a day. What are the must-see landmarks?”

“Recommend 8 places of interest in Singapore for a solo traveler.”

“I want to visit Tokyo. What are some famous sightseeing spots?”

Medium (7–13)

“I’m going to Rome and I love history. Recommend places focused on ancient architecture.”

“I’ll be in Bangkok with my parents. Suggest calm, less walking-heavy places.”

“I have 6 hours in Istanbul near the city center. Create a short list of places I can realistically cover.”

“Suggest places in New York that are great for photography—street scenes, skyline views, and unique neighborhoods.”

“I’m visiting Barcelona but I want to avoid tourist traps. Give me underrated places locals love.”

“In London, suggest places that combine museums + parks + a nice evening walk route.”

“I’m going to Jaipur in summer. Suggest indoor-friendly places plus 1–2 evening outdoor spots.”

Hard (14–20)

“Plan places of interest in Tokyo for 3 days: Day 1 modern city vibes, Day 2 traditional culture, Day 3 nature/quiet spots. Keep travel time minimal.”

“I’m traveling to Mumbai with a 4-year-old. Suggest places that are kid-friendly, stroller-friendly, and have clean restrooms nearby.”

“I want a ‘food + culture’ itinerary in Istanbul: places near famous food streets, plus historical landmarks. I’m vegetarian and want options.”

“I have a knee injury. For Rome, suggest places of interest that minimize stairs, include rest points, and are still iconic.”

“I’m visiting Iceland in winter. Suggest places of interest that are safe to access, include geothermal experiences, and maximize chances of seeing the Northern Lights.”

“I want a 5-day trip in Japan but I hate crowded places. Recommend places of interest across two cities that are quieter, and explain why each is low-crowd.”

“I’m flexible on destination. Suggest 3 destinations in Southeast Asia for a 7-day trip that fits: beaches + light hiking + strong local culture + budget-friendly + good public transport. Then list top places of interest for each destination.”
