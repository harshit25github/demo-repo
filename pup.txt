ProposedAIUseCase
We plan to add an AI assistant inside FPCLOUD that works like a smart guide for every team. Instead of searching through menus or asking the RPA team, users can simply type or ask in plain English: “Who owns this service?”, “Help me create a new VM”, or “How do I onboard a new project?”. The AI will instantly answer questions, show related information, and guide users step-by-step through actions like creating, updating, or managing services. This makes FPCLOUD easy to use for everyone, reduces dependency on RPA, and enables true self-service across the organization.
