cheapoair.ai is a smart trip-planning chat assistant that remembers just the important bits about each customer (budget, style, diet, strong likes/dislikes) and uses them to tailor every reply. By feeding the model only a few, highly relevant notes each turn, it delivers a usable itinerary faster, reduces “off-target” answers, and lowers our AI cost per conversation. We track simple business outcomes—conversion to itinerary/booking, time to first plan, CSAT, error rate, and cost per chat—to show clear lift. I designed and built the backend: the agent logic, the lightweight memory layer (profile + short notes), and the dynamic context pipeline that makes the assistant personal, reliable, and efficient.
