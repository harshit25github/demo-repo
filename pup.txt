We are building a Natural Language Business Insights Assistant that allows anyone in the organization to query multiple data sources—like booking databases, Google Analytics, and other logs—using plain English. Instead of just returning SQL results, the system provides end-to-end analysis and context, connecting the dots between operational data and business performance, enabling faster and smarter decision-making.

Key Areas

Business Pain Points

Data is fragmented across SQL, GA, and logs.

Non-technical users depend on analysts for every query.

Time-to-insight is slow, often days instead of minutes.

Resolution

Users ask natural questions → AI maps them to pre-defined safe tools.

Results are not just raw queries, but insightful narratives (e.g., “Success % improved due to fewer GDS failures, but GA shows a drop in traffic which may impact bookings.”).

Strict read-only tools ensure safety and reliability.

Impact

Faster decisions: Reduce insight generation from days → minutes.

Productivity: Analysts/engineers freed from repetitive queries.

Self-service: Empower leadership and business teams to explore data safely.

Future Extensions

Support other DBs (Postgres, MySQL, Snowflake, NoSQL).

Add domain-specific tools for KPIs (conversion, funnel drop-offs).

Create a unified conversational layer for all enterprise data.
