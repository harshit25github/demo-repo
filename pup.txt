A smart chat assistant that plans trips. It remembers key details about a customer (preferences, past choices) so the plan feels personal and accurate—without re-asking the same questions.

Why it matters:

Customers get to a usable itinerary faster and feel understood.

More people move from “browsing” to booking.

We spend less on AI usage because we only pass the most relevant info to the model.

Fewer “off” answers → fewer escalations and better satisfaction.

Business Impact (what improves)

Conversion: More chats result in itineraries and bookings because suggestions match the customer’s style and budget.

Speed to value: First draft itinerary arrives sooner; less back-and-forth.

Cost efficiency: Slimmer prompts = fewer tokens = lower AI bill per chat.

Customer experience: Higher CSAT/NPS; the assistant “remembers” (e.g., “prefers boutique hotels,” “vegetarian,” “avoids red-eye flights”).

Reliability: “Remind me what we discussed earlier” now works—less frustration and fewer handoffs.

What changed under the hood (non-technical)

We keep a small profile of stable facts (budget level, dietary needs, home airport).

We keep short memory notes from earlier chats (e.g., likes hiking; disliked hostels in Bali).

On every message, we feed the assistant only 3–4 most relevant notes + today’s request.

This keeps answers personal and on-topic while keeping costs low.

Key Metrics to track (plain language)

Conversation → Itinerary rate
% of chats where the customer gets a proposed plan.

Itinerary → Booking rate
% of itineraries that become bookings.

Time to First Itinerary (TTFI)
Minutes from first message to the first plan shared.

Customer Satisfaction (CSAT/NPS)
Simple thumbs-up score or survey.

Wrong/Off-target Answer Rate
% of responses flagged by QA or customers.

Cost per Conversation
Our AI cost per chat (tokens x price).

“Memory Used” Rate
% of turns where past preferences actually helped the answer.

Response Time (p95)
How fast replies come back for customers.

My role — What I did

Architecture & backend: Designed and implemented the end-to-end agent runtime, context assembly, and APIs.

Memory system: Built PROFILE (KV) + VECTOR (mem0/Redis) with min-score + top-k + recency fallback; added atomic fact writes to avoid noise.

Agent integration: Wired async instructions builder, prompt strategy, and guardrails (temperature, dedupe, truncation).

Infra & diagnostics: Set up RediSearch vector indexes (DIM=1536), persistence, and health checks (module/version + VECTOR capability scripts).

Quality & reliability: Reduced hallucinations and token bloat via retrieval thresholds, rerank by similarity/recency, and deterministic context feeds.
