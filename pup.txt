Enhance **Manager V2's orchestration intelligence** to fully leverage our three specialists: **Flight Agent, Hotel Agent, and Trip Planner**.

### 1. Intelligent Multi-Agent Coordination

Currently, the Manager is not optimally utilizing the capabilities of all three specialists.

We need the Manager to intelligently understand the user's complete travel requirements and coordinate specialists whenever they can provide meaningful value.

**Example:**
User: "Plan a comfortable 7-day trip to Paris, preferably with nonstop flights, a centrally located hotel, and a $1,500 budget."

Expected behavior:
- **Trip Planner:** Create an itinerary aligned with the user's preferences.
- **Flight Agent:** Search flights and intelligently apply relevant filters, such as stops, airlines, baggage, timings, duration, and price.
- **Hotel Agent:** Search hotels and apply relevant filters, such as location, ratings, amenities, price, and other supported criteria.
- **Manager:** Coordinate these operations, preserve shared constraints, and deliver one cohesive recommendation.

### 2. Explore Complete Specialist Capabilities

Thoroughly inspect **all existing tools, filters, schemas, prompts, context, and feedback mechanisms** across Flight, Hotel, and Trip Planner.

Understand how the Manager can intelligently:
- Identify relevant user preferences, including implicit and multi-turn requirements.
- Pass appropriate criteria to each specialist without losing context.
- Leverage available search and filtering capabilities rather than stopping after initial results.
- Coordinate dependencies between agents, including destinations, dates, budgets, locations, and travel preferences.
- Reuse existing searches and apply filters when appropriate instead of unnecessarily restarting searches.
- Adapt recommendations based on actual specialist results and constraints.

**Do not limit this improvement to budget handling. Explore every useful capability already supported by our specialists.**

### 3. Improve Manager Intelligence

- Identify current routing and coordination gaps.
- Enhance Manager prompting, specialist instructions, and feedback where necessary.
- Encourage meaningful multi-agent collaboration without blindly invoking every specialist.
- Handle missing information intelligently without inventing required search criteria.

### 4. Hotel Response Styling

Investigate why Hotel Agent responses are missing appropriate icons.

Verify existing client-side SVG hydration and backend-generated Unicode icons. Improve visual presentation with subtle, contextual icons without duplication.

### 5. Implementation & Testing

Create challenging multi-turn scenarios involving different combinations of flight filters, hotel preferences, itinerary requirements, and changing user constraints.

Inspect actual routing, tool payloads, filter execution, context updates, and final responses.

Implement improvements, fix failures, and rerun tests sequentially.

**Goal:** Make Manager V2 a sophisticated travel orchestrator that intelligently combines **all available specialist capabilities, search criteria, and filters** to deliver the most relevant, personalized travel experience—not simply invoke multiple agents.
