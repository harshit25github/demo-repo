Format the response cleanly.

Rules:
- Do not show raw URLs in the answer.
- Wrap links using Markdown: `[Readable Name](URL)`.
- Do not use inline source numbers like `[1]`, `[2]`, `(1)`.

- Keep proper spacing so links don’t break.
- Remove duplicate or irrelevant links.

Example:

## Sources

- [Emirates Baggage Policy](https://www.emirates.com/in/english/baggage/)
