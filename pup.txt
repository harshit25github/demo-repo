FPCLOUD is our internal cloud portal, like a Google Maps for company software and infrastructure. It centralizes all services, APIs, databases, and VMs with clear ownership, documentation, and connections, giving teams a single place to discover, manage, and deploy resources. Instead of relying on scattered tools or tribal knowledge, FPCLOUD provides a standardized, transparent view of the entire ecosystem and is already deployed across the organization.

The challenge is that FPCLOUD is currently used mainly by the RPA team to handle deployments for others, while most teams struggle to adopt it directly. Lack of awareness, training needs, and complexity of fields mean that onboarding and CRUD/VM tasks still depend on RPA support. This creates bottlenecks and prevents true self-service. By adding an AI layer that explains the portal in plain English, guides users step-by-step, and answers context-specific questions, FPCLOUD can evolve into a self-service assistant—empowering every team to onboard, manage entities, and deploy resources on their own, reducing training overhead and freeing RPA capacity
