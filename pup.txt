We can add guardrails for clear cases like prompt injection, off-topic queries, abusive content, and internal system exposure.

But complex travel-safety queries like “Is this destination safe?” or “Is this a disputed area?” should not be blocked directly. These are valid travel concerns and need proper advisory context.

Since we are using a nano model for blocking, the guardrail should stay lightweight and focus on classification/routing. Adding every scenario into it will increase cost, latency, and complexity, without guaranteeing full coverage.

Recommended approach: use guardrails for clear block/allow decisions, and route travel-risk queries to a dedicated advisory flow with trusted sources.
