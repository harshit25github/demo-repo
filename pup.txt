This project builds a grounded knowledge system that crawls airline and CheapOair baggage/policy pages, indexes them, and retrieves the most relevant evidence before answering user questions.
It is designed to give reliable, citation-backed answers for baggage rules, privacy policy, and terms instead of forcing users to manually search multiple pages.

Key metrics are retrieval accuracy, grounded answer rate, route-scope accuracy, linked-policy resolution rate, and response latency; the current evaluated accuracy is about 90% on the benchmarked set.
The business impact is faster customer answers, lower support effort, better trust because answers are evidence-based, and easier reuse across environments through saved crawl snapshots.
In short, better retrieval quality directly improves user experience, reduces wrong answers, and increases operational reliability.
