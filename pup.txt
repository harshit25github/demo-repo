Investigate and plan improvements to our **Human Handoff Guardrail**, including potential migration to the **OpenAI Decisions API**.

### Existing Problem — Inconsistent Human Handoff

Our current guardrail behaves inconsistently in multi-turn conversations.

**Example:**
1. User: "I need to speak to someone live."
2. Agent: Performs a soft handoff because search criteria are missing.
3. User: "No, I still want to speak to someone before purchasing."

**Issue:** Sometimes the guardrail correctly triggers human handoff on the second request, but other times it fails or repeats the soft handoff. This behavior is inconsistent and needs to be resolved.

### Expected Behavior

- **Search criteria available:** Direct human handoff.
- **Search criteria missing:** Soft handoff on the initial request.
- **User insists after soft handoff:** Always trigger human handoff, regardless of search criteria.

### Your Task

1. Investigate the root cause of the inconsistency in the existing guardrail.
2. Explore migrating the classification logic to the **OpenAI Decisions API**, similar to our Post-Booking Guardrail.
3. Evaluate how conversation history, previous soft handoffs, and repeated user intent should influence classification.
4. Determine whether explicit handoff-state tracking is necessary.
5. Compare the current implementation with a Decision API approach for accuracy, consistency, and latency.
6. Design challenging multi-turn test cases and recommend the simplest reliable solution.

**Important:** R&D and planning only. Do not implement yet. Avoid unnecessary wrappers or hardcoded logic.

**Expected Output:** Root-cause analysis, recommended Decision API integration design, affected files, and testing plan.

**Goal:** Eliminate inconsistent human-handoff behavior, especially when users repeatedly request a live agent after receiving a soft handoff.
