FPCLOUD is our internal cloud management portal designed to bring every part of our technology ecosystem together in one place. It acts as a single window where teams can discover, manage, and deploy all services, APIs, databases, and virtual machines with clear ownership and documentation.

The portal simplifies how teams handle infrastructure by offering a standardized structure for entity creation, updates, and deployments. Instead of relying on multiple tools or manual coordination, FPCLOUD provides complete visibility across systems—showing dependencies, relationships, and usage details in an organized and transparent way.

Already deployed and actively used by the RPA team for managing deployments, FPCLOUD ensures consistency, compliance, and efficiency across environments. It helps reduce operational overhead, improves collaboration between teams, and lays the foundation for a unified, scalable approach to managing infrastructure and services across the organization.
