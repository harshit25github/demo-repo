Enhance our Next.js chat client to handle acknowledgement messages with a natural streaming animation.

Requirements

1. Handle status: "ack" events

Acknowledgement chunks are sent only by the Manager approach, not Gateway.

When the client receives a chunk with status: "ack" and a non-empty message, display that acknowledgement.

Gateway should continue working exactly as before, without requiring acknowledgement handling.

2. Simulate streaming on the client

The backend sends the complete acknowledgement in a single chunk.

Instead of displaying it instantly, progressively reveal the text character-by-character or in small chunks with a slight delay.

Make the animation smooth, natural, and responsive, without introducing unnecessary latency.

3. Message lifecycle

Display the acknowledgement as a temporary assistant message.

Once the actual assistant response starts streaming, smoothly replace the acknowledgement with the real response.

Prevent duplicate messages, overlapping animations, or mixed content.

Handle cancellation, errors, and interrupted streams properly.
